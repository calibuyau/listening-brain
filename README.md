# Listening Brain

Companion to the poster *Tuning music to brain networks: a quiet-scanner fMRI feasibility study for precision music-based neuromodulation* (SFRMBM 2027, Clermont-Ferrand). Point a phone at the poster figure "Listen to this figure" to hear the music and see brain networks follow it, or open the 3D view (`?mode=3d`).

Data: large-scale network time courses from one consenting volunteer (ICube, Université de Strasbourg, CNRS), shown on the fsaverage5 template brain. No anatomical or identifying data.

Libraries: three.js (MIT), MindAR (MIT), in `lib/`.
