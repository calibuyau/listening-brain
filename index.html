Listen to this figure

Point your phone at the figure on the poster. You will hear the music our volunteer heard in the scanner, and see each brain network follow it, measured with fMRI on three hearings.

  1. 1Sound on, or headphones.
  2. 2Tap Start and allow the camera.
  3. 3Frame the figure “Listen to this figure”. Try the other pieces at the bottom.

The camera image is analysed on your phone and never recorded or sent. One healthy volunteer, research data, not a clinical tool.
